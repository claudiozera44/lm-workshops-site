Questions?

Frequently asked

Who should attend? +
Software engineers, architects, and CTOs building or considering entry into the London Market. No prior London Market experience required—workshops accommodate all levels from complete beginners to experienced practitioners.
How are workshops delivered? +
Workshops are delivered both on-site (London) and online via live video. Sessions offer hands-on labs, code reviews, and live Q&A. Recordings are provided for reference.
What's included in the fee? +
All materials, documentation, code examples, and templates. Recordings of all sessions. 30 days of email-based follow-up Q&A support.
Can you customize a workshop for our team? +
Yes. Bespoke workshops are tailored to your team's context, timeline, and maturity level. Contact us to discuss your specific needs.
What do I need before attending? +
A basic understanding of REST APIs and backend systems. If attending advanced workshops, familiarity with async patterns and distributed systems will be helpful. All prerequisites are outlined when you enroll.
What's your cancellation policy? +
Full refund if cancelled more than 21 days before the workshop. 50% refund if cancelled 7–21 days before. No refund if cancelled within 7 days.
